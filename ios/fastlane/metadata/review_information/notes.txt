ZKProofport generates zero-knowledge proofs on-device.

To test the app:
1. Tap "Connect Wallet" and sign in via Privy (email or social login)
2. The app requires a Coinbase-issued EAS attestation on Base network to generate proofs
3. Without a valid attestation, the proof generation screen will show but cannot complete a proof

Note: This app performs cryptographic proof generation locally on the device.
No server-side processing is involved for proof generation. The app communicates
with blockchain networks (Base/Ethereum) for attestation verification only.
