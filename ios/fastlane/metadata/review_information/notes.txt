ZKProofport generates zero-knowledge proofs on the device itself. Nothing about
a proof is computed on a server.

HOW TO TEST

The two entries on the first "Verify" screen read an attestation that Coinbase
issues on the Base network:

  Coinbase KYC Verification
  Coinbase Country Verification

Both need a wallet that already holds such an attestation, so they cannot be
completed with a brand-new test wallet. Tapping either one is still worth doing:
the screen connects a wallet (WalletConnect), reads the chain, and then explains
what is missing rather than failing silently — which shows the flow and the
error handling without an attestation in hand.

If you have a wallet holding a Coinbase attestation on Base, connect it and tap
"Generate Proof". The proof is built on the device; a progress list names each
step, and it takes roughly 30-60 seconds on an iPhone. Nothing about the wallet
or the attestation leaves the device except the finished proof.

THE THIRD PROOF, WHICH YOU CAN COMPLETE WITHOUT AN ATTESTATION

The picker at the top of the Verify screen selects a network. Switching it from
"Base" to "Other" reveals:

  OIDC Domain

This one needs no on-chain attestation. It proves that you belong to a work
email domain (a Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 organisation) without
revealing the address itself. You type a domain, sign in with Google or
Microsoft, and the app builds the proof on the device from the identity token
they return.

No ZKProofport account exists and none is created. The sign-in is Google's or
Microsoft's own, against whatever work account you already have; we never see a
password. If you do not have a work account to hand, the two Coinbase entries
above still demonstrate the flow.

The remaining networks in that picker ("Giwa", "OmniOne") are hidden unless
Developer Mode is switched on, and are not part of this submission.

WHAT THE APP DOES WITH THE NETWORK

Proof generation is local. The app reaches the network for two things:
downloading the public verification circuits, and reading attestations from the
Base and Ethereum networks. There is no server-side proof service.
