This build redesigns Verify, Wallet, History, and More, and presents dApp proof conditions and EIP-712 actions as cards before signing. This internal test build uses production services.

1. Search and select organization account, Coinbase KYC, and country proofs in Verify. Check that Laboratory opens GIWA, Arc, and Korean mobile ID flows.

2. Request a proof through a dApp QR code or deep link. Check the requested conditions and action type, then tap the chevron to inspect the original field names, values, and nested details. Compare them with the signature request shown by MetaMask.

3. Complete a proof, use Done or Generate Another to return to the catalog, and start a new proof. Report a frozen screen or repeated previous request. Also check accepting, cancelling, and returning to the requesting dApp.

4. Check wallet connection status and circuit wallets. Open History to inspect saved conditions, actions, generation status, and verification results. Records from older builds may have no saved request details.

5. Switch between Korean and English and between light and dark themes in More. Reopen the app and confirm the choices persist. Without a manual language selection, the app should follow the phone language.

The default tabs are Verify, Wallet, Scan, History, and More. Coinbase proofs require a wallet with the corresponding attestation. When reporting an issue, include the screen, proof type, and reproduction steps.
