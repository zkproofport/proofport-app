This build adds Korean.

1. On a phone set to Korean, the app should come up in Korean.
   If it comes up in English, that is the defect — please say so. Switching
   the phone to English should give you an English app, and that is correct.
   One exception: if you picked a language yourself under More > Language on
   an earlier build, that choice is remembered and still wins over the phone.
   Pick again in the same place to change it.

2. Are there five tabs — Verify, Wallet, Scan, History, More?
   A sixth called OpenStoa, between Scan and History, means the wrong build
   shipped. Say so if you see it.

3. Make one proof end to end.
   On the Verify tab, switch Network at the top from Base to Other and OIDC
   Domain appears. Sign in with an ordinary Google account and leave the domain
   box empty. The proof is built on the phone and takes 30-60 seconds.

The two Coinbase entries need a wallet holding a Coinbase attestation on Base.
Without one, reaching the explanatory message is the correct outcome.
