1. RECORDING
Attached: physical iPhone, latest iOS, from app launch. All three proofs are
generated end to end, then the Wallet, History and More tabs.

None of the three items you listed apply - see below.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
ZKProofport proves a fact about its user without disclosing the data behind it.

The normal flow: a dapp asks for a proof through our SDK; the request opens
this app; the proof is generated ON THE DEVICE; the finished proof returns to
the dapp through our relay, and the dapp verifies it on-chain against a
verifier contract, or off-chain. Nothing else leaves the phone. The
Verify tab also makes any proof with no dapp, which is what the recording
shows.

- Coinbase KYC: the user passed Coinbase's identity check
- Coinbase Country: their country of residence is, or is not, in a given list
- OIDC Domain: they hold an email address at a given domain, without revealing
  the address

For services needing an assurance about someone, and people who would rather
prove it than hand over documents. A proof takes 30-60 seconds; there is no
proof server.

3. HOW TO TEST
No accounts exist, so there are no credentials to issue.

OIDC Domain needs no wallet and no company account - an ordinary personal
Google account does it:
  1. Verify screen: Network picker from "Base" to "Other"
  2. Choose "OIDC Domain"
  3. Leave "Domain to prove" empty, leave Scope as it comes
  4. Verification mode: "Any Email" - the one that takes an ordinary Google
     account; the other two want an organization account
  5. "Sign in with Google"
  6. The proof builds on the device; a progress list names each step

The two Coinbase entries (Network = "Base") read an attestation Coinbase issues
on Base, so only a wallet already holding one can complete them - they may not
be completable for you. Both are demonstrated in the recording.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Reown AppKit over WalletConnect: the only wallet mechanism. No embedded
  wallet, no custody; keys never leave the user's own wallet.
- Base, read-only, via public RPC endpoints (mainnet.base.org and three other
  public providers) and the attestation index base.easscan.org: reads what
  Coinbase published. No transaction is sent.
- Google (accounts.google.com), Microsoft Entra
  (login.microsoftonline.com/organizations): identity token for OIDC Domain.
- GitHub, read-only (raw.githubusercontent.com/zkproofport/circuits): the
  public proving circuits and keys, checksummed before use.
- relay.zkproofport.app: carries a finished proof back to the dapp that asked
  for it. Not involved when a user proves something on their own.

No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK, and no AI service.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Identical in every region; nothing is gated by country or storefront. The
only variation is interface language, chosen by the user.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
We perform no identity verification and are not a financial service. The
Coinbase proofs read what Coinbase already
published on Base, a public network, needing no agreement with them. Google and
Microsoft authenticate through their own sign-in. No protected third-party
material.

7. NO ACCOUNTS, NO USER CONTENT, NO PURCHASES
No registration, password or profile exists. Connecting an external wallet is
the authenticated state; signing in with Google is Google's own sign-in against
an account the user already holds, so we never see a password and create
nothing. No account can be created, so there is no deletion flow.

What the user can delete is what the app stored on the phone: History > Clear
All for the proof records, More > Clear Local Data for the rest, and More >
Export Proof History hands it back first. Shown in the recording.

No posts, comments, messages or profiles - nothing one user does is visible to
another, so there is nothing to report or block. Nothing costs money.
